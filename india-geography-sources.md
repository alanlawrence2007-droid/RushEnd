# India geography references

The selector uses a curated set of Indian states, union territories, and representative district options. These references were consulted to avoid inventing the national geography structure:

- Government of India state/UT district directory: https://igod.gov.in/sg/district/states
- Government of India India facts page for states and districts: https://www.india.gov.in/explore-india/facts-of-india/states-ut-districts
- Census of India population finder, including India/state/district hierarchy: https://censusindia.gov.in/census.website/data/population-finder
- Humanitarian data geoboundaries for India, including ADM1 and ADM2 boundaries: https://data.humdata.org/dataset/geoboundaries-admin-boundaries-for-india

Implementation note: this frontend remains a static demo and does not claim exhaustive live administrative coverage. The landing selector is India-first, with state/UT choices and representative districts that scope the existing queue visualization until a full geography API or boundary dataset is connected.
